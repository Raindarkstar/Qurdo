# Qurdo Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 9, 2026

## English

### 1. Who We Are

Qurdo is developed and operated by raindark.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, you may contact the developer through the support contact listed on Qurdo's App Store page or through the project's GitHub repository.

### 2. Data We Collect

Qurdo does not collect, upload, sell, or share any personal user data.

The app does not require an account, does not use advertising tracking, and does not use analytics services to monitor user behavior.

Tasks, dates, notes, completion status, and reminder settings created inside Qurdo are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to the developer's server.

### 3. Local Storage and Notifications

Qurdo uses local device storage to save your tasks and review data so the app can restore them when you open it again.

Qurdo may request notification permission if you enable task reminders. These reminders are scheduled as local system notifications based on the date and reminder time you choose. If you complete or delete a task before the reminder time, the reminder is canceled.

Qurdo uses Expo and React Native libraries to support app functionality such as local notifications and storage. Qurdo does not use Expo push notification tokens or a remote notification server to collect or send your task data.

### 4. How We Use Data

Because Qurdo does not collect user data, we do not use personal data for advertising, profiling, analytics, or third-party sharing.

Local task data is used only to provide the app's core features, including task management, daily review, reminders, and local persistence on your device.

### 5. Data Sharing

Qurdo does not share user data with third parties.

Apple may process information related to App Store downloads, purchases, crash reports, or system-level services according to Apple's own policies. That processing is controlled by Apple, not Qurdo.

### 6. Your Rights and Choices

You control the task data stored in Qurdo. You can edit or delete tasks inside the app at any time.

You can disable notification permission for Qurdo in your device settings. You can also delete the app from your device to remove locally stored app data.

If you have questions, requests, or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the support contact listed on Qurdo's App Store page or the project's GitHub repository.

### 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Qurdo's features or data practices change. If the policy is updated, the "Last updated" date above will be changed.

## 中文

### 1. 谁在提供本 App

Qurdo 由 raindark 开发和运营。

如果你对本隐私政策或数据处理方式有任何问题，可以通过 Qurdo 在 App Store 页面列出的支持联系方式，或通过项目的 GitHub 仓库联系开发者。

### 2. 我们收集什么数据

Qurdo 不收集、不上传、不出售、也不共享任何用户个人数据。

本 App 不需要注册账号，不使用广告追踪，也不使用分析服务来追踪用户行为。

你在 Qurdo 中创建的任务、日期、备注、完成状态和提醒设置会保存在你的设备本地，不会发送到开发者服务器。

### 3. 本地存储与通知

Qurdo 使用设备本地存储来保存你的任务和复盘数据，以便你下次打开 App 时继续使用。

如果你开启任务提醒，Qurdo 可能会请求系统通知权限。提醒会根据你选择的任务日期和提醒时间，以本地系统通知的方式发送。如果你在提醒时间之前完成或删除该任务，对应提醒会被取消。

Qurdo 使用 Expo 和 React Native 相关库来支持本地通知、数据存储等 App 功能。Qurdo 不使用 Expo 推送令牌，也不通过远程通知服务器收集或发送你的任务数据。

### 4. 数据使用目的

由于 Qurdo 不收集用户数据，我们不会将个人数据用于广告、画像、分析或第三方共享。

本地任务数据仅用于提供 App 的核心功能，包括任务管理、每日复盘、提醒和设备本地保存。

### 5. 数据共享

Qurdo 不会与第三方共享用户数据。

Apple 可能会根据其自身政策处理与 App Store 下载、购买、崩溃报告或系统服务相关的信息。这些处理行为由 Apple 控制，不属于 Qurdo 的数据收集。

### 6. 用户权利与选择

你可以控制保存在 Qurdo 中的任务数据，并可以随时在 App 内编辑或删除任务。

你可以在设备系统设置中关闭 Qurdo 的通知权限。你也可以从设备上删除 App，以移除本地保存的 App 数据。

如果你对本隐私政策有疑问、请求或投诉，可以通过 Qurdo 在 App Store 页面列出的支持联系方式，或通过项目的 GitHub 仓库联系开发者。

### 7. 政策更新

当 Qurdo 的功能或数据处理方式发生变化时，我们可能会更新本隐私政策。政策更新后，顶部的“最后更新日期”也会同步更新。
